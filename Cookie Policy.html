Cookie Policy

Last updated: 4/16/2026

This Cookie Policy explains how AdOnverse uses cookies and similar technologies on its website and platform.

1. What Are Cookies

Cookies are small text files placed on your device when you visit a website or use an online service. They help websites function, remember settings, improve security, and understand service performance.

2. Why We Use Cookies

We use cookies and similar technologies to:

  • keep the website and platform functioning properly
  • maintain session continuity and login state
  • remember basic preferences where available
  • improve reliability, security, and technical performance
  • understand how users interact with the website and platform
  • improve campaign flow, gameplay quality, and overall user experience

3. Types of Cookies We May Use

a) Strictly Necessary Cookies

These cookies are necessary for basic platform functionality, such as authentication, security, session management, and essential service delivery.

b) Performance and Analytics Cookies

These cookies help us understand how users interact with the website or platform so we can improve product quality, usability, and performance.

c) Preference Cookies

These cookies help remember language, display settings, or similar choices where available.

4. Third-Party Technologies

Some cookies or similar technologies may be set by approved service providers supporting analytics, hosting, security, embedded services, or technical infrastructure.

Where required by law, we will request consent before using non-essential cookies.

5. Your Choices

Depending on your location and the technologies in use, you may manage cookies through:

  • our cookie banner or cookie settings tool, where available
  • your browser settings
  • device-level privacy controls

Please note that disabling strictly necessary cookies may affect site functionality.

6. Legal Basis & Updates

Where required by applicable law, strictly necessary cookies may be used to provide essential requested services, while non-essential cookies may be used only on the basis of consent or another lawful basis where applicable.

7. Updates

We may update this Cookie Policy from time to time. When we do, we will update the "Last updated" date above.

8. Contact

Active Recall

Players don't just see your ad. They study it.

Passive ads are forgotten the moment they are skipped. In AdOnverse, we turn your brand's messaging into core gameplay mechanics.

Through the "Sponsor Memory" feature, players are quizzed on details of your campaign. To earn crucial Bonus Points, they must actively read, understand, and recall your brand's value proposition.

"This shifts the dynamic entirely. Users are no longer trying to ignore the sponsor; they are actively seeking out the sponsor's information to win the round."

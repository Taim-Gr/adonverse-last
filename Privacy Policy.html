Privacy Policy

Last updated: 4/16/2026

AdOnverse respects your privacy and is committed to protecting personal data in accordance with applicable law, including Turkish data protection rules and other laws that may apply depending on where users are located.

This Privacy Policy explains what data we may collect, how we use it, how we protect it, and what rights you may have.

1. Who We Are

AdOnverse is an interactive advertising platform that enables sponsor-backed, skill-based campaign experiences.

For privacy-related questions, contact: info@adonverse.com

2. Data We May Collect

Depending on how you use the platform, we may collect:

  • Account information such as email address, username, region, language, or similar registration details.
  • Technical information such as device type, operating system, browser type, IP address, and approximate geolocation derived from IP.
  • Usage and gameplay data such as session activity, challenge completion, progression, timing, interaction behavior, rankings, and reward eligibility events.
  • Campaign interaction data such as whether a sponsor challenge was entered, completed, revisited, or progressed through.
  • Support and communication data when you contact us.
  • Limited redemption-related information where necessary to support sponsor-backed reward fulfillment through approved external providers.

3. Pseudonymous and Internal Identifiers

To reduce unnecessary exposure of personal identity, AdOnverse may use internal references, pseudonymous identifiers, or server-generated user IDs for campaign measurement, gameplay analysis, progression tracking, and reward eligibility evaluation.

This means certain performance and campaign data may be processed through internal identifiers rather than through direct disclosure of your personal identity to sponsors.

4. How We Use Personal Data

We may use personal data to:

  • create and manage accounts
  • operate, secure, and maintain the platform
  • run skill-based campaign experiences and gameplay systems
  • evaluate points, progression, participation, rankings, and eligibility outcomes
  • support sponsor-backed reward flows
  • improve platform quality, stability, fairness, and user experience
  • produce reporting and analytics
  • prevent abuse, fraud, manipulation, and unauthorized access
  • comply with legal obligations
  • protect our legal rights

5. Sponsor Reporting

AdOnverse may provide sponsors with reporting about campaign activity and performance.

Where possible, such reporting is aggregated, anonymized, pseudonymized, or based on internal platform identifiers rather than direct personal identity.

We do not provide sponsors with unnecessary personal identity data merely to show campaign performance.

6. Legal Bases

Where required by applicable law, we rely on lawful bases such as:

  • your consent, where consent is required
  • necessity to provide services requested by you
  • compliance with legal obligations
  • legitimate interests, where permitted and balanced against your rights
  • necessity for the establishment, exercise, or protection of legal rights

7. Sharing of Personal Data

We may share personal data only where necessary and appropriate, including with:

  • service providers that support hosting, infrastructure, analytics, customer support, security, or technical operations
  • approved external reward fulfillment providers where necessary for sponsor-backed redemption flows
  • sponsors only where necessary for lawful campaign operation, reporting, fraud control, or reward administration
  • regulators, authorities, courts, or law enforcement where required by law
  • advisers, auditors, or transaction counterparties in connection with financing, restructuring, merger, acquisition, or sale, subject to appropriate safeguards
We do not sell your personal data to advertisers.

8. International Transfers

Your personal data may be processed or transferred across borders where required for platform infrastructure, analytics, reward fulfillment, technical operations, or support.

Where required by law, we apply appropriate safeguards for such transfers.

9. Data Retention

We retain personal data only for as long as necessary for the purposes described in this Privacy Policy, including platform operation, security, legal compliance, dispute resolution, auditing, and record-keeping.

Retention periods may vary depending on the type of data and the legal or operational reason for keeping it.

10. Data Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures designed to protect personal data against unauthorized access, loss, misuse, alteration, or unlawful disclosure.

However, no online platform or transmission method can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

11. Your Rights

Subject to applicable law, you may have rights such as:

  • the right to know whether your personal data is being processed
  • the right to request information about that processing
  • the right to request correction of inaccurate or incomplete data
  • the right to request deletion or destruction where legal conditions are met
  • the right to object to certain types of processing where permitted by law
  • the right to request compensation where unlawful processing causes harm, where provided by law

To exercise your rights, contact: info@adonverse.com

12. Children

AdOnverse is not intended for unlawful or unauthorized use by children where parental permission or legal capacity is required.

13. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we do, we will update the "Last updated" date above.

14. Contact

For privacy requests or questions, contact: info@adonverse.com

Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 5, 2026

AdOnverse respects your privacy and is committed to protecting personal data in accordance with applicable data protection laws, including Turkish data protection requirements and other laws that may apply depending on where users are located.

This Privacy Policy explains what information AdOnverse may collect, how we use it, when it may be disclosed, how we protect it, and the choices and rights available to users.

1. Who We Are

AdOnverse is an interactive advertising and engagement platform that enables users to participate in sponsor-backed, skill-based campaigns, games, challenges, and reward experiences.

For privacy-related questions or requests, contact: info@adonverse.com

2. Information We Collect

Depending on how you use AdOnverse, we may collect the following categories of information:

Account Information

We may collect information necessary to create, authenticate, and manage your account, including:

  • name
  • email address
  • internal or server-generated user ID
  • authentication information provided through supported sign-in methods

When Google Sign-In or another OAuth-based authentication method is used, authentication may be handled through the relevant authentication provider.

Approximate Location

We may determine your country or approximate location using information such as your IP address.

We use approximate location to operate country-specific platform functionality, including determining which campaigns and reward options are available in a user's current market.

AdOnverse does not require precise GPS location for this purpose.

Usage and App Interaction Data

We may collect information about how users interact with AdOnverse, including:

  • game and challenge participation
  • campaign interactions
  • session activity
  • progression and milestone completion
  • points and performance
  • timing and completion information
  • reward eligibility events
  • other actions performed within the platform

This information is used to operate the platform, measure performance, maintain campaign integrity, provide analytics, and prevent fraud or manipulation.

Device and Technical Information

We may collect technical information such as:

  • device or other identifiers
  • device type
  • operating system
  • browser or application information
  • IP address
  • technical events required for platform operation, security, advertising, fraud prevention, and performance measurement

Reward and Redemption Information

We may process information relating to rewards, including:

  • reward eligibility
  • reward status
  • available or reserved balances
  • issued or pending rewards
  • redemption history
  • limited information necessary to process a reward request

When claiming a reward, users may be asked to provide the destination information required for that specific redemption. This may be different from the email address associated with the user's AdOnverse account.

Where an external reward fulfillment provider is used, only information necessary to fulfill the requested reward may be processed for that purpose.

3. Internal and Pseudonymous Identifiers

AdOnverse may use internal references, pseudonymous identifiers, device identifiers, or server-generated user IDs to operate the platform and reduce unnecessary exposure of users' direct identities.

These identifiers may be used for:

  • account and platform functionality
  • campaign participation
  • gameplay and progression tracking
  • reward eligibility
  • analytics and performance measurement
  • advertising operations
  • fraud prevention and security

Where practical, campaign performance information is processed using these identifiers rather than directly identifying information.

4. How We Use Information

We may use information collected through AdOnverse to:

  • create, authenticate, and manage user accounts
  • provide platform and application functionality
  • display and operate sponsor-backed campaigns and advertising experiences
  • determine campaigns available to users based on their market
  • determine reward options available for a user's market
  • operate games, challenges, milestones, points, and progression systems
  • determine participation, performance, rankings, and reward eligibility
  • process and administer reward requests
  • analyze platform usage and performance
  • improve platform functionality, reliability, and user experience
  • maintain campaign fairness and integrity
  • detect and prevent fraud, abuse, manipulation, and unauthorized activity
  • protect users, sponsors, and AdOnverse
  • comply with applicable laws and regulatory obligations
  • establish, exercise, or defend legal rights

5. Advertising and Sponsored Campaigns

AdOnverse contains advertising and sponsor-backed campaign content.

Advertising within AdOnverse may include interactive campaigns, sponsored challenges, branded experiences, promotional content, or other sponsor-funded activities.

We may use information such as approximate location, app interactions, internal identifiers, and device or technical information where necessary to deliver campaigns, measure campaign performance, maintain campaign integrity, prevent abuse, and operate advertising functionality.

AdOnverse does not sell users' personal data to advertisers.

6. Campaign and Sponsor Reporting

AdOnverse may provide sponsors with information about the performance of their campaigns.

Campaign reporting may include information such as participation, completion, engagement, performance, milestones, and other campaign results.

Where appropriate, reporting is provided in aggregated, anonymized, pseudonymized, or internally identified form.

We do not provide sponsors with users' direct personal identity merely for the purpose of reporting campaign performance.

7. Service Providers and Disclosure of Information

AdOnverse may use service providers to support functions such as:

  • hosting and infrastructure
  • authentication
  • technical operations
  • analytics
  • security and fraud prevention
  • customer support
  • reward fulfillment

These providers may process information only where necessary to provide the relevant service or functionality.

For reward fulfillment, users may provide destination information specifically for the requested reward. Where an external fulfillment provider is required to deliver that reward, the information necessary for fulfillment may be processed for that purpose.

We may also disclose information where required by law, legal process, regulators, courts, or competent authorities, or where reasonably necessary to protect our legal rights, users, platform, or services.

We do not sell personal data to advertisers.

8. International Data Processing

Because AdOnverse and its service providers may operate across different countries, information may be processed or transferred internationally where necessary for platform infrastructure, authentication, analytics, security, technical operations, support, or reward fulfillment.

Where required by applicable law, appropriate safeguards are used for international transfers.

9. Data Retention

We retain personal data only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes described in this Privacy Policy.

Retention periods may depend on the type of information and the reason it is processed.

Certain information may be retained where necessary for:

  • legal or regulatory obligations
  • security and fraud prevention
  • accounting or auditing requirements
  • dispute resolution
  • enforcement of agreements
  • establishment, exercise, or defense of legal claims

When information is no longer required, it may be deleted, anonymized, or otherwise handled in accordance with applicable law.

10. Account and Data Deletion

Users can permanently delete their AdOnverse account through the Delete Account functionality provided by AdOnverse.

Users may also initiate account deletion through the designated AdOnverse account deletion webpage.

Account deletion removes the user's account and associated account data, subject to information that AdOnverse may be required or permitted to retain for legal, regulatory, security, fraud-prevention, accounting, auditing, or dispute-resolution purposes.

Users should be aware that deleting an account may result in the loss of remaining balances, rewards, progression, campaign history, and other account-related information.

For additional privacy or deletion requests, users may contact: info@adonverse.com

11. Data Security

AdOnverse uses reasonable technical and organizational measures designed to protect personal information against unauthorized access, misuse, loss, alteration, or unlawful disclosure.

Data transmitted between users and AdOnverse is protected using appropriate encryption in transit where applicable.

However, no internet-based platform or electronic transmission method can guarantee absolute security.

12. Legal Bases for Processing

Where applicable law requires a legal basis for processing personal information, AdOnverse may rely on:

  • performance of services requested by the user
  • consent, where consent is required
  • legitimate interests, where permitted and appropriately balanced against users' rights
  • compliance with legal obligations
  • protection of users, the platform, and other parties against fraud or abuse
  • establishment, exercise, or defense of legal claims

The applicable legal basis may depend on the type of information, processing activity, and user's jurisdiction.

13. Your Privacy Rights

Depending on your location and applicable law, you may have rights relating to your personal information, including the right to:

  • know whether personal information about you is being processed
  • request information about such processing
  • request access to certain personal information
  • request correction of inaccurate or incomplete information
  • request deletion or destruction where applicable legal conditions are satisfied
  • object to certain processing where permitted by law
  • withdraw consent where processing is based on consent, subject to applicable limitations
  • exercise other rights provided by applicable data protection law

To exercise applicable privacy rights, contact: info@adonverse.com

14. Children

AdOnverse is not intended for use by children where such use would be prohibited by applicable law or would require parental or guardian authorization that has not been obtained.

Age requirements and restrictions may vary depending on the user's jurisdiction.

15. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when our platform, practices, legal obligations, or services change.

When this Privacy Policy is updated, the Last updated date at the top of this page will be revised.

Users are encouraged to review this Privacy Policy periodically.
